How do you want to use MobSimLab?

Use on this computer

Everything runs on this computer: the database, the simulations and your projects. No account and no sign-in. Nothing leaves this computer unless you export it.

Connect to a server

Work with your team on your organisation's MobSimLab server. You sign in with its single sign-on.

You can change this later in Settings. Projects move between the two by exporting and opening the project file (.mslab).
